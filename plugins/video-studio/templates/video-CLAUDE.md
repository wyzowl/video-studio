# A Wyzowl Video Studio video

This video is made with Wyzowl Video Studio and rendered with HyperFrames on this Mac.

- Work on it through the **video-studio:production** skill and the skills it brings in, with the plugin's guides for
  how things are made. HyperFrames' own skills aren't part of Video Studio.
- Run HyperFrames as the production skill's `HF` (Video Studio's version, with its two switches) or through this
  folder's `npm run` scripts, which name the version the video was made with. Never run `npx hyperframes` without a
  version: npx would fetch the newest release.
