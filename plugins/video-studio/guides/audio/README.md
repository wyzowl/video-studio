# Sound

A mix is a set of relationships, not a stack of effects: two tracks that each sound right alone can fight together,
and the fix is rarely "turn one down", but finding what they're fighting over and giving it to whichever needs it.

| When you're… | Read |
|---|---|
| Levelling the stems, ducking the music under a voice, cleaning a voice, adding effects | `mixing.md` |
| Adding sound effects from SFX Kit | `sfx.md` |
| Choosing, editing or making music | `music.md` |
| Timing words, cuts and sounds to the voice and the music | `sync.md` (the Sync standard) |

The numbers (loudness, peaks, levels) are in `../numbers.md`, "Sound". Where sound comes from, by default: the
voiceover is the user's file; music is the user's track or one made with the score kit; sound effects come from SFX
Kit; the logo's sound from the brand kit. Anything else (a track from a library, an AI voice, a sound from a website)
is used only when the user asks for it and supplies it, under its own terms.
