# Techniques

How single moves are built, one family to a file: a short note for each move, with its numbers. Find a move by its
job below, or by name (`grep -rn "^### " .`). The shapes of whole scenes they combine into are in `../patterns/`;
why things move and how several move together, in `../principles.md`, `../timing.md` and `../choreography.md`.

A scene is usually two to four moves, one of them its signature, all drawn from the time alone (`../../format.md`).
Building a move from these notes beats starting from a whole pattern when nothing in `../patterns/` fits.

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `text.md` | typing, swapping, arrivals, decoding, emphasis, exits, handwriting |
| `camera.md` | zoom and pan, zooming into an off-centre target, phases with drift, following a point, 3D flight, a tilted page, crash zoom, dolly zoom, whip, shake |
| `cursor.md` | clicks, presses, drag and drop, several people at once, a control and its target, a detection box, finding where to point |
| `layout.md` | groups arriving (pop in, bursts, clouds, mosaics), rings and networks, tilted cards, containers growing, morphing and swapping, collisions, shaped reveals |
| `data.md` | count-ups, bars, progress, ratings, one layout for every figure, reading a chart |
| `effects.md` | lines drawing themselves, living icons, light, focus, motion blur, glitch, bursts, hits, holds, moving grounds, motion from the music |

## By job

| Job | Moves |
|---|---|
| A word or line arriving | `text.md`: Words on their spoken times, Waterfall, Line mask, Wipe with a light edge, Outline to fill, A vocabulary of entrances; `effects.md`: Coming into focus |
| A hit on the beat | `text.md`: Beat slam; `effects.md`: Impact punch, Flash cut, Cuts on the beat, Glitch; `camera.md`: Crash zoom, Shake |
| Pointing at one thing | `text.md`: Marker effects, Spotlight; `effects.md`: Depth of field, Glow behind a hero; `camera.md`: Two wrappers |
| The product working | `cursor.md`; `text.md`: Smooth type-on, Human typing, with edits; `layout.md`: A panel that grows |
| Many things | `layout.md`: Pop in, Burst from the centre, Pack into a mosaic, A network round a hub, Orbit, Assemble from a 3D cloud |
| A comparison | `layout.md`: Two tilted cards, Swap at the same place; `data.md`: Bars growing |
| A figure | `data.md`; `effects.md`: Burst lines, Particles |
| A change of state | `layout.md`: A container that morphs, Swap at the same place, Card flip, The whole look changes, one thing stays; `text.md`: Hard-cut swap, Slot roll |
| Moving through a space | `camera.md`: One wrapper, A camera in phases, with drift, 3D flight, A page as a tilted card, Whip |
| The brand appearing | `effects.md`: Lines drawing themselves; `text.md`: Metallic sheen, Rays from a wordmark; `layout.md`: Iris open |
| A celebration | `effects.md`: Particles, Burst lines, Glow behind a hero |
| A long hold | `effects.md`: Living icons, A status that shimmers, Life on a held shot; `camera.md`: A camera in phases, with drift |
| Following the music | `effects.md`: Motion from the music; `layout.md`: A palette that flips on the beat; `text.md`: Word grid |

Loud moves (colour splits, glitches, neon, electric arcs, burst lines, flash cuts, cuts on the beat, shake) suit
energetic films; a calm or premium film builds from line masks, coming into focus, depth of field, a single sweep
of light and slow camera moves. The film's personality decides (`../principles.md`).
