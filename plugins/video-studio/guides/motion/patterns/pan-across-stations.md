# Pattern: pan across stations

Stations laid out on one oversized canvas, visited in turn by one camera that pans to centre each and reveals its
label, landing held on the last. 7–10 s; each stop about a second.

**Beats**
1. The camera opens on the first station, its label revealing, then starts the pan to the next.
2. Each pan (eased both ways) centres the next station, whose label or callout reveals on arrival.
3. The last pan lands on the final station; its callout reveals and the camera holds still on the punchline.

**Shapes**
- **A timeline:** evenly spaced markers along a thin line, panned one way, each with a callout that springs up from
  its pointer, ending on today.
- **A tangle:** stations scattered in two dimensions, linked by rough drawn lines the camera follows diagonally,
  ending on a scribbled knot (too many disconnected steps).
- **Concept, then demo:** two shots joined by one sideways pan with parallax: a phrase whose key word decodes, then
  a live demo.

**Keep:** one camera on one world (no cuts), the stations placed in the world from the start, pans of the same kind.

**Techniques:** a world-wrapper pan between targets, callout spring pops, drawn lines (`../techniques/camera.md`,
`../techniques/text.md`).
