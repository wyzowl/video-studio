# Pattern: a typed line

A caret types a line as a person would, then the line collapses into the brand, or it types under a standing mark.
"Someone is typing this" is the engine. 3.5–7 s.

**Beats**
1. *Typing (to about 2 s).* On a plain ground, a caret blinks at the line's start, then the line types on letter by
   letter behind it. It can sit inside an input pill whose whole assembly slides left to keep the caret centred as
   the words run off the left edge.
2. *The edit (about 2–4.5 s).* The line changes in place: the last word backspaces out and a better one is typed,
   or the last word snaps to the accent colour. Under a standing mark, the line beneath is cut or wiped away instead.
3. *Resolve.* As a hook: the caret goes, the line collapses to a point (squashed sideways or scaled to nothing), and
   the brand springs in where it was (the mark, then the wordmark sliding out from behind it; or an interface
   control a cursor clicks). As an ending: the mark has stood still above the whole time, and the ask types into the
   line beneath it, perhaps as a button in the accent colour, with a soft halo settling round the mark.

**Keep:** smooth, human typing (pauses at punctuation, a correction that means something), the mark never leaving
the frame in the ending version. The camera stays still.

**Techniques:** type-on with caret, backspace and retype, collapse and spring pop (`../techniques/text.md`).
