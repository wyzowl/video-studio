# Pattern: data hero

Figures and charts are the hero, and the camera travels through them to land on one number, so the data carries the
argument. 4–12 s. Real figures with their source only (`../../design/data.md`).

**Shapes**
- **Push through:** a ring sweeping to a percentage with its number counting up, then a push straight through its
  centre into a chart that draws left to right, then a pan to a final card. For a problem that's large or worsening.
- **Scroll to the hero:** a tilted grid of real charts and cards scrolls by itself until the hero figure glides into
  the centre and glows, while a one-word-at-a-time headline lands beside it; a push in, then out to a settled wide.
- **Burst:** a dark, empty frame; a few icons punch in at the centre, then the headline figure grows huge as the
  icons fling outward; a slow lean-in.
- **Montage:** short headline beats cut between charts drawing themselves and a dashboard a cursor scrubs (a line
  following it, readouts updating).
- **One gauge** as a single beat inside a run of words.

**Beats (push through)**
1. The first instrument: the number counts up while it grows to its final size, its ring or bar landing on the same
   ease, as one event.
2. The camera travels to the next instrument, which resolves.
3. The hero card lands dead centre, a soft glow behind it, and holds.

**Keep:** one instrument per beat; the camera carries the cut; tabular figures so counting digits don't jitter;
nothing bouncy on serious figures.

**Techniques:** count-ups with scale, rings and bars, chart draws, scrub readouts (`../techniques/data.md`,
`../techniques/camera.md`).
