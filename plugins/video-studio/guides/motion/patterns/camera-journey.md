# Pattern: camera journey

The camera is the storyteller: a journey of several legs through one continuous world, each leg motivated by
what happened or what it lands on. 5.5–11 s.

**Two shapes**
- **Cause and effect.** Dive into a panel, an action fires (a click, a message sent), and the camera swoops to where
  the consequence appears ("do this here, get that there").
- **A flight.** A cinematic flight with motion blur and depth of field over content that acts by itself (a dropdown
  choosing, a card coming into focus), ending on a sudden push onto the hero or the ask.

**Beats**
0. *Optional still prologue,* broken by a cut or by the headline slipping away as the first dive begins.
1. *Dive in:* fast and tight onto the focal element (flat, or at an angle with the neighbours blurred).
2. *The hinge:* the camera holds or drifts while the content acts; the action is what motivates the next leg.
3. *Travel to the consequence:* pull back, swoop or pan to where the result builds (bars sinking into a line,
   a row arriving, a receipt expanding); or rotate the world flat into an overview.
4. *Land:* come to rest on the changed state, or dive hard onto the ask and hold (even mid-dive).

**Keep:** vary the legs (four identical pushes read as a slideshow); dives and landings on strong ease-outs, the
moves between on `power2.inOut`, never a springy camera; every target measured once at build; the moving world
marked `data-layout-allow-overflow`.

**Techniques:** multi-leg camera, target zoom, motion blur, depth of field (`../techniques/camera.md`,
`../techniques/effects.md`).
