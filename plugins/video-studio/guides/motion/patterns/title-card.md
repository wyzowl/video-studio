# Pattern: title card

The calm beat: one clean card, revealed with exactly one restrained move, then a still hold. The stillness is the
point. 3–5 s; a chain of cards 5.5–9.5 s.

**Beats**
1. The opening state: an empty ground, or a busy frame that's about to be wiped away.
2. The one move: a line fading in while it scales slightly (95% to 100%); or a large pill sweeping diagonally across,
   wiping the busy frame away to reveal a clean lockup.
3. The hold: the card stays, perhaps with a second line sliding up to replace the first. At most one quiet live
   element, or a very slow drift.

**A chain:** two or three cards in a row, each arriving with at most one move and holding, joined by instant hard
cuts at full opacity (or a blur that snaps into focus), ending on the logo held to the last frame. A grey-to-bright
append ("Name" gaining "2.0") or a type-on counts as the one move.

**Keep:** don't add a second development: it's a breather. If the cards flip in under a second each, it's
`words-in-beats`.
