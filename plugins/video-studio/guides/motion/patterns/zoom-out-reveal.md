# Pattern: zoom-out reveal

Open tight on one full-frame detail, let it act in close-up, then one continuous decelerating pull-back shows it was
part of something bigger; the frame locks and the story carries on inside the wide. 7–11 s.

**Beats**
1. *The detail (to about 2.5 s):* a graphic or a small part of an interface fills the frame with no surrounding
   chrome, and performs (a shape morphs or blooms; rows pop in, a highlight steps down).
2. *The middle:* either the pull-back is already running and passes through an intermediate level (a big headline,
   a card on a page), or the close-up develops at the same tightness.
3. *The reveal:* one zoom-out, heavily decelerated (`expo.out`), to the whole: a workspace, a page, a product. It
   ends before the scene does.
4. *After the lock:* everything else is element motion inside the wide: a cursor arriving, a playhead scrubbing, a
   panel expanding, a long hold on the result.

**Keep:** the only camera move is outward, once; no push-in anywhere, nothing moves the camera after the lock. Build
the whole world at its final layout and start the camera scaled in on the detail (4–12×); anything visible in the
close-up must be sharp at that scale (vector, or images at that resolution).

**Techniques:** world-wrapper zoom (`../techniques/camera.md`).
