# Pattern: takeover

A phrase types in, one word cycles through options ("this could be many things"), then the hero crashes in from off
the frame and physically shoves the words aside: "actually, it's this". A collision, not a fade. 5–7 s.

**Beats**
1. *Context (to about 1.4 s):* the lead-in types on, smoothly, with confidence.
2. *The cycle (about 1.4–3 s):* the accent word rolls through two or three options (more reads as filler).
3. *The collision (about 3–4.2 s):* the hero arrives with momentum and pushes the whole line out of the way; the words
   react to the impact rather than fading. The hero lands heavy: a longer settle, reading as mass.
4. *Alone:* the hero settles centred and holds still.

**Keep:** the camera still; the displacement happens between the elements. As an ending, the brand's mark is the hero.

**Techniques:** type-on, a slot roll, displacement on impact (`../techniques/text.md`, `../techniques/layout.md`).
