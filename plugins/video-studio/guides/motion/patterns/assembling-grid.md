# Pattern: assembling grid

Many items (tiles, cards, logos, list lines) cascade into a grid or a list and hold: "look how much it does", "look
who uses it". 3–10.5 s.

**Beats**
1. *Arrivals (to about 1 s):* an empty grid region; items arrive in a quick stagger (0.04–0.08 s apart; a list about
   one a second), each fading and sliding or scaling a short way into its own slot. No scatter, no big bounce:
   overshoot only on small markers. A headline may fill in above, with any figure counting up.
2. *Resolve and hold:* the last items land; the array holds, resting (at most a slow push or a light passing
   across).
3. *Finish,* one of: hold; a closing line; the array clearing to one payoff line (a claim, a price, an address); a
   push through one highlighted item; or a pull-back showing the array inside something far bigger.

**Shapes**
- **A list that accumulates:** each line arriving with a marker pop, a tick drawing and its text wiping in; or the
  stack stepping up a slot per beat so the newest sits in the bright focus slot; or a fast stream past a focus slot
  that slows to stop on the chosen item.
- **A board that fills itself:** grey placeholders filling and swapping to real values, cards springing in tethered
  to markers on a map, statuses flipping after it's built: "it's doing it now".
- **A wall of logos (the user's own partners, with permission):** built, then a pull-back to show the scale.

**Keep:** for dense walls (8 or more), items slide straight into their own slots rather than bursting from a shared
centre.

**Techniques:** staggered arrivals, tick draws, mask wipes, count-ups, fills (`../techniques/layout.md`,
`../techniques/data.md`).
