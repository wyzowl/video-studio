# Scene patterns

Proven shapes for a whole scene: what happens across its full length, beat by beat, with one signature move. The
storyboard can name one for a scene (the scene's plan says "pattern: words-in-beats"), keep its signature move and
fill its slots with the video's own content; or compose a scene from techniques (`../techniques/`) when none fits.
Never force a pattern on a beat it doesn't suit.

Each pattern paces its reveals across the scene, timed to the words, so the scene keeps developing instead of
freezing; that's what makes them worth starting from. They're shapes, not looks: the brand kit dresses them, and
the library's items can fill them.

## By the scene's job

| Job | Patterns |
|---|---|
| Hook | `words-in-beats`, `typed-line`, `pan-across-stations`, `hub-and-satellites`, `mark-to-button` (as an opener), `takeover`, `fixed-anchor`, `prompt-and-answer`, `cursor-demo` (many hands at work), `data-hero` (one figure bursting), `zoom-out-reveal` |
| The problem | `words-in-beats`, `pan-across-stations`, `data-hero`, `closing-in` |
| Introducing the product | `words-in-beats`, `logo-lockup`, `cursor-demo`, `data-hero`, `video-to-figure`, `pan-across-stations`, `prompt-and-answer`, `device-showcase`, `title-card` |
| A key feature | `assembling-grid`, `cursor-demo`, `device-showcase`, `side-by-side`, `video-to-figure`, `data-hero`, `prompt-and-answer`, `machine-at-work`, `live-edit`, `camera-journey`, `scroll-to-deliverable` |
| Benefits | `words-in-beats`, `assembling-grid`, `title-card`, `camera-journey`, `zoom-out-reveal`, `fixed-anchor`, `cursor-demo` |
| Proof | `hub-and-satellites`, `assembling-grid`, `title-card`, `data-hero` |
| The ask | `words-in-beats`, `logo-lockup`, `mark-to-button`, `title-card`, `hub-and-satellites`, `prompt-and-answer` |
| The end | `words-in-beats`, `typed-line`, `logo-lockup`, `takeover`, `fixed-anchor` |

## The patterns

| Pattern | In a line | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `words-in-beats` | Bold words on a plain field; the motion is the words changing, in place or beat by beat | 3–13 s |
| `typed-line` | A line typed (and corrected) as a person would, then collapsed into the brand, or typed under a standing mark | 3.5–7 s |
| `pan-across-stations` | Stations laid out on one big canvas, visited by one camera panning from stop to stop | 7–10 s |
| `camera-journey` | The camera itself tells the story: dive in, something happens, travel to the consequence, land | 5.5–11 s |
| `zoom-out-reveal` | Open tight on a detail, then one decelerating pull-back shows what it was part of | 7–11 s |
| `hub-and-satellites` | Nodes spring into a ring round a centre, then the shot resolves on the centre | 5–8 s |
| `assembling-grid` | Many items cascade into a grid or list to show breadth, then hold | 3–10.5 s |
| `logo-lockup` | The mark comes into being on screen and resolves into the lockup | 4.5–11 s |
| `cursor-demo` | A cursor drives the product's interface through its states | 4–13 s |
| `device-showcase` | A device or window held as the hero while its screens move through a real flow | 5–11 s |
| `prompt-and-answer` | A request types into a real input, and the product answers | 5–12 s |
| `machine-at-work` | One trigger, then the product visibly working, then the receipt ticking off | 4–11.5 s |
| `live-edit` | A control and the thing it controls change together, in the same frame | 5–12 s |
| `scroll-to-deliverable` | A long page of work scrolls past as evidence, then one action reveals the result | 5–12 s |
| `data-hero` | Figures and charts are the hero; the camera travels through them to one number | 4–12 s |
| `title-card` | One calm card, one restrained move, then stillness | 3–5 s (chains 5.5–9.5 s) |
| `side-by-side` | Two equal things arrive from opposite sides, mirrored, and hold | 4–6 s |
| `closing-in` | Things pile up until the viewer is surrounded | 6–10 s |
| `takeover` | Options cycle, then the hero crashes in and shoves them aside | 5–7 s |
| `fixed-anchor` | One thing stays dead still while everything around it changes | 6.5–11 s |
| `video-to-figure` | A clip plays, slides aside for a big figure, then gives way to words | 6–8 s |
| `mark-to-button` | The brand's mark condenses into a button, and a cursor clicks it | 4–6 s |
