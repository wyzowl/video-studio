# Pattern: closing in

Overwhelm by accumulation: things the viewer recognises pile up until they feel surrounded. Recognition, then
claustrophobia. For "you're buried", not "this figure is bad" (that's `data-hero`). 6–10 s.

**Beats**
1. *Recognisable things assemble:* three surfaces the viewer knows (generic ones, never another company's real
   product), the centre one full size, the sides smaller, each floating slightly.
2. *Density:* icons scatter in around them, as markers of how much there is.
3. *The turn:* the centre surface empties and reshapes, revealing a person underneath: the viewer.
4. *Close in:* demands close in from every side towards the person, who stays still. The camera holds: the feeling
   comes from being surrounded, not zoomed into. Hold on the crowd.

**Another ending:** the clutter piles up (notes, windows, alerts, text still typing inside them) under a slow
pull-back, then the camera pushes in and the clutter is shoved to the edges, opening space for a question that
builds word by word.

**Techniques:** staggered arrivals, a container morph, radial entries, displacement (`../techniques/layout.md`).
