# Pattern: logo lockup

The brand's mark comes into being on screen and resolves into the lockup, sometimes carrying on to the address or
the ask. 4.5–11 s. The mark itself is always the brand kit's own file; the build moves its real parts.

**Ways in**
- **Built from parts:** the mark's own shapes arrive and interlock (a dot traces a circle, halves overlap, strokes
  turn in), then the wordmark slides out from behind it.
- **Drawn on:** the stage clears, and the mark draws itself stroke by stroke before the wordmark reveals.
- **Bloomed:** words clear to an empty frame, and the whole mark springs up from nothing at the centre, then slides
  aside as the wordmark reveals.
- **Morphed:** the line before it changes in place and transforms, without a cut, into the mark.
- **Settled:** the lockup is there from the first frame; things around it drift away and an underline sweeps under
  the wordmark.
- **A system around a still mark:** rings pulse and shapes orbit a mark that never moves, under one slow camera tilt.

**Then**
- The lockup holds still, centred; or a push through the mark's negative space streaks the ask past the camera and
  lands on the address; or the address or a button wipes in beside or below it.
- The finished lockup holds dead still for a long stretch (up to half the scene), the camera included: no drift on
  the last shot.
- Centre the mark by what's visible, not by its file: padding in an image, or a mark heavier on one side, puts the
  file's centre off the mark's. Measure the visible bounds on a still and place by those.

**Keep:** the mark's geometry, colours and spacing exactly; no squash, spin or blur on it unless the brand is
playful; the brand kit's ident sound on the moment it lands (`../../audio/sfx.md`).

**Techniques:** stroke draw-on, staggered letters, spring bloom, mask wipes (`../techniques/text.md`,
`../techniques/effects.md`).
