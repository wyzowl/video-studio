# Pattern: hub and satellites

Labelled nodes spring into a ring round a centre, then the scene resolves on the centre: "it connects everything",
"it sits at the centre of your work". 5–8 s; as an end card, about 2.5 s.

**Beats**
1. The main nodes (an icon and a label each) spring in, staggered, in a wide ring round an empty or marked centre.
2. Smaller nodes fill the gaps; thin connectors or an orbit ring may draw from the centre to each.
3. The resolve, one of:
   - **Push into the centre:** the camera pushes in; the inner nodes grow sharp while the outer ones slide to the
     edges and blur; hold on the core.
   - **Orbit:** the brand's mark lands in the centre and the satellites turn round it, upright, under a slow pull-back.
   - **Collapse:** a click on the empty centre implodes the ring, and the product springs out of it as the answer.
   - **Scatter:** no ring; many icons pop in across the frame round a headline, then drift slowly outward.

**Keep:** one centre; the satellites' order and timing from a seeded spread, so it never looks random by accident.

**Techniques:** spring pops, connectors drawing, orbit, push with depth of field (`../techniques/layout.md`,
`../techniques/camera.md`).
