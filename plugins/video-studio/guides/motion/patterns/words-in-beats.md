# Pattern: words in beats

Bold words on a plain field, where the motion is the words changing. The workhorse: reach for it whenever the words
carry the scene and there's no set, no product surface and no click. 3–13 s.

**Two shapes**
- **One line, one slot changing.** A fixed line holds while one word swaps by hard cut, rolls through a list, or is
  deleted and retyped ("Plan your **week** / **launch** / **wedding**"). The rest of the line never moves.
- **A statement built across beats.** Each full-frame beat replaces the last, each with its own entrance (a scale
  pop, letters tumbling in from depth, a fly-in that resolves sharp, the last line zooming past the camera, tracking
  closing up, a masked rise), building to one payoff.

**Beats**
1. *First beat (to about 1 s).* The first word or line lands dead centre with one entrance: typed on behind a
   caret, a hard flash in, a word-by-word blur-in, or an oversized word scaling down. An accent move may mark the
   key word: an underline drawing on, a small burst behind it, a box framing it.
2. *The engine.* Beats replace each other in place, nothing lingering from the last. The background may flip
   between two of the brand's grounds, the type inverting with it.
3. *Resolve and hold.* The last beat lands and stays: a punctuation snap, a payoff element springing in, the logo or
   the address forming, or the brand's one defining word. The final hold is the longest beat, up to half the scene
   for an end card.

**Tempo**
- Staccato: 8–12 short phrases under half a second each, for breadth and energy.
- Relay: 2–4 statements, each built its own way and held 1.5 s or more, for weight.
- A beat's payload can be one non-text element (a chart, a button, the mark) obeying the same arrive, hold, clear.

**Techniques:** text swaps and type-on, per-word reveals, beat slams, scramble decodes, marker effects, spring pops,
depth scatter (`../techniques/text.md`, `../techniques/effects.md`). The camera stays still, or pushes slowly under
the whole run.
