# Pattern: video to figure

A clip of the product plays in the centre, then slides aside to hand its weight to a big figure in the space it
leaves, then both clear and words type into the middle, sealed by a pill. Show, yield, pivot, stamp. 6–8 s.

**Beats**
1. *The clip (to about 1.6 s):* the product footage lands centred on a smooth scale-up and plays.
2. *Yield (about 1.6–3.2 s):* the clip slides aside (moving and shrinking) into the space the figure now fills as the
   figure pops in: one weight transfer, read as one event.
3. *Pivot (about 3.2–5 s):* both clear, and the impact line types into the empty centre, its accent words carrying
   what the clip showed.
4. *Stamp:* a pill in the accent closes around the line (`scaleX` 0 to 1), a glow resolving a beat behind it. Hold.

**Keep:** the clip slides, never cuts; each exit pairs with an entrance at the same place, so it reads as two beats,
not four; the camera still. Footage plays muted unless its sound is the point.

**Techniques:** count-ups, type-on (`../techniques/data.md`, `../techniques/text.md`).
