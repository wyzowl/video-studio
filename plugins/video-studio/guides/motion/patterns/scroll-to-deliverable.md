# Pattern: scroll to the deliverable

The frame travels down one long page of generated work (a transcript, a task feed, a document) as evidence, until one
interaction turns it into the result. "It did all this, and here's what you get." 5–12 s.

**Beats**
1. *Traverse (about the first half):* a small opener (a title typing, a header docking to the corner), then the
   page moves past in reading order: prompt, steps, ticked items, a summary. One slow pan, a fast stream, or stepped
   scrolls that slow at each stop.
2. *The hinge,* one interaction: a file chip pops in and is clicked; a sentence is highlighted and a pill pops above
   it; a row reaches the centre and expands; or the summary finishing is the trigger.
3. *The result:* one fast decelerating pull-back to the whole workspace (the page becomes a sidebar beside the
   result), a document window growing to fill the frame and settling on its highlighted cells, an inline panel
   opening below the line, a row unfolding into its steps. Then the frame locks.

**Keep:** at most two real camera moves, around the hinge; scroll the page or pan the camera, never both at once;
the moving layer marked `data-layout-allow-overflow`; the work shown is real.

**Techniques:** page scrolls, highlights, expanding rows, pull-backs (`../techniques/layout.md`,
`../techniques/camera.md`).
