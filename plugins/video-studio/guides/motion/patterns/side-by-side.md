# Pattern: side by side

Two equal things shown together: they arrive from opposite sides with mirrored tilts, like a book opening, and hold
as a balanced pair. For an A and a B, or two abilities that work together; not for three or more (`assembling-grid`)
or for steps. 4–6 s.

**Beats**
1. *The concept (to about 0.8 s):* a centred title with an accent word settles down into place from just above.
2. *The split (about 0.4–1.9 s):* the two cards arrive from the left and the right, the second about 0.2 s behind,
   each turned on `rotationY` towards the centre and growing from 0.85 to full as it lands, overlapping the title's
   end so it reads as one arrival. Shadows fall outward.
3. *Punctuation:* a badge lands on each card's inner edge, a third of a second apart, overlapping the card a little;
   the only overshoot in the scene. Hold.

**Keep:** each side its own colour identity (a faint glow behind each), one perspective for both, the camera still:
the symmetry is the subject.

**Techniques:** mirrored tilts, spring pops (`../techniques/layout.md`).
