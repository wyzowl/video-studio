# Pattern: fixed anchor

One element enters once and never moves again (the brand's name, a product's input, a lead line) while everything
around it changes. The anchor's stillness is the claim: everything changes, this stays. 6.5–11 s.

**Beats**
1. *The anchor lands and pins (to about 2 s):* it enters once, centred, and never drifts, breathes or re-lays out.
2. *The cycle (to about 70% of the scene),* either:
   - **beside it:** a slot next to the anchor steps through states: a label slapped over by the next, a highlight
     walking along a line, a fast vertical carousel. Steady (0.5–1 s a state), or starting slow and accelerating to
     a blur (12–16 states read as "everyone"; 3–8 as a roll-call). It never touches or covers the anchor.
   - **around it:** the whole setting re-skins in place every second or so (colours, type, corners, chrome
     cross-fading together) while the anchor's content stays identical.
3. *The emphasis:* the cycle resolves rather than just stopping: the whole line lights up, the flurry halts on the
   weightiest phrase, the setting mutes.
4. *The lockup:* a last element joins the anchor (a closing word, the brand's sign-off on a shared baseline), and the
   finished composition holds for a fifth to a third of the scene.

**Keep:** the camera completely still; no push "for energy".

**Techniques:** text swaps, highlight steps, carousels, theme cross-fades (`../techniques/text.md`,
`../techniques/layout.md`).
