# Card: the review

The eight standards on a page, for the review skill: read it once a session in their place, and score each from its
checklist and its scale. Open the full standard when a score sits on the line (a 7 or an 8), and to find a fault's
fix in its "What lowers it". Judge as agency-grade work (`../README.md`, "Agency-grade"): a film that only avoids
faults, with plain frames and a still world, isn't an 8. What the checks measure is fixed, not scored.

## Hook (`hook`): `../story/hooks.md`

Watch the first 3 seconds cold, as someone scrolling past. The first frame is already composed and something moves or
reads within a second; the promise is clear by 3 s (1.5 s vertical): tension, curiosity or desire, in the viewer's
language, never the company introducing itself; the words and picture make the same move; the film pays it off.
9–10 stops a scroll, its promise clear by 3 s and kept · 8 strong and clear, could land a beat sooner · 6–7 clear but
ordinary, or a slow first second · 4–5 slow, generic or about the company · 1–3 no hook.

## Reads at a glance (`phone`): `../design/readability.md`

On the key sheet, at a phone's width: every word that matters reads; each line holds its reading time and something to
take in holds long enough; few words, big; strong contrast against what's really behind them; still while read;
clear of the platforms' bands, captions and faces; one thing to read at a time, the voice and the words agreeing.
9–10 every word reads at a glance, in time, without effort · 8 one line a touch small or quick, still reads · 6–7 some
words need a second look · 4–5 several lines don't read on a phone or vanish first · 1–3 much can't be read.

## Motion (`motion`): `../motion/quality.md`

On the strips, frame by frame, and the seams either side: one personality from the kit's motion; every move has
weight (arrivals settle on a long tail, big moves wind up, the world reacts) and a job (it reveals, orders, answers,
explains or celebrates); one main action at a time, the most important first; the world alive between moves; it reads
at full speed, once; seams invisible, speeds matched; clean, with no stutter or frozen frames.
9–10 weight, a job and one personality in every move, invisible seams · 8 a couple of moves could land better · 6–7
generic: default eases, little secondary action, a dead world, a seam that jumps · 4–5 distracting faults · 1–3
broken.

## Variety (`variety`): `../motion/variety.md`

Down the key sheet: something new every few seconds (an element, a framing, a reveal, a camera move, a cut); no device
used twice, each idea shown its own way; variety inside the film's personality; contrast of pace (the slowest scene
about three times slower than the fastest, held moments placed on purpose); a few kinds of transition, each with a
reason.
9–10 something new every beat, pace that rises and falls · 8 varied, with one repeat or one flat stretch · 6–7 a
device reused, a stretch with nothing new, one transition doing most of the work · 4–5 mostly the same move or layout ·
1–3 a slideshow.

## Composition (`composition`): `../design/layout.md`

Each key frame as a still: where does the eye land first, then next? One focal point; deliberate balance; margins and
safe areas kept at every size, with room for overshoot; filled with purpose, not crowded (the hero big); depth and the
film's own world; one system across the film; every size laid out for its shape.
9–10 every frame composed like a still, in a rich world · 8 strong, one frame a little crowded, empty or off balance ·
6–7 plain, web-like or generic in places · 4–5 cramped, empty or covered frames, no system · 1–3 things off the frame.

## Brand accuracy (`brand`): `../design/brand.md`

The kit's colours exactly, in their roles; its fonts at its weights; the real logo, with its space; its motion
identity (eases, durations, entrance); its grounds and graphic language; its voice; its real things (product, screens,
photos), never redrawn from imagination.
9–10 unmistakably the brand, at its best · 8 on brand, with a small slip (a colour role, a weight, a looser logo space) ·
6–7 the brand's colours and logo, but generic in look or motion · 4–5 off-brand in places, or invented assets · 1–3
not the brand.

## Sync (`sync`): `../audio/sync.md`

With the sound on, around key words and cuts: key words land on the spoken word (from 0.15 s before to 0.05 s after);
cuts at phrase gaps; with music, scene changes on bar lines and hits on beats; with neither, pacing by reading time;
each sound on the frame of its moment; nothing drifting by the end.
9–10 every key word, cut and sound lands exactly · 8 in sync throughout, one moment a little loose · 6–7 noticeable late
words, loose cuts or sounds off their moment · 4–5 often apart, cuts mid-phrase, music ignored · 1–3 out of sync.

## Story (`story`): `../story/principles.md`

One message a viewer could repeat, stated by the second beat; the opening's promise paid off by the close; benefit-led,
the product shown where it matters; flowing beat to beat, not a slideshow; one clear ask with the brand; the right
length.
9–10 one message anyone would repeat, the promise paid off, every beat earns its place · 8 clear and well shaped, one
beat drifts or the ending could land harder · 6–7 diluted: a feature list, a slow start, an ending that doesn't pay
off · 4–5 several messages, or the source's order · 1–3 no message a viewer could name.
