# Cut-downs

Several short videos cut from one long recording (a talk, an interview, a webinar, a podcast with video): a footage
video type, made from the recording's transcript. Each short stands alone.

## Finding the moments

Read the verified transcript (`../../cli.md`, "Sound and speech") for passages that work on their own:
- a claim and its proof;
- a short story with a turn;
- one step or tip, complete;
- a strong opinion, a surprising fact, a vivid example;
- a question and its answer.

Each has a clear beginning that needs no context ("As I said…" or "So, the second thing…" don't start a short), one
idea, and an ending that lands. Propose more candidates than needed, each with its time range, its one-line idea and
its proposed first line, and let the user choose.

## Cutting each short

- **Open on the strongest line.** The first words carry the hook (`../hooks.md`, The Cold Open); if the best line
  comes later in the passage, it can open the short and then the passage plays from the start. Never open on a
  greeting or a throat-clearing.
- **Keep the speaker's meaning.** Cut pauses, false starts and repetitions, but never join words so that the speaker
  says something they didn't mean, and never move a sentence away from what it answered.
- **Cut at word boundaries,** with short fades at every join (`../../footage/editing.md`).
- **Length:** 15–60 seconds each, usually 20–45; one idea each.
- **Shape for the platform:** usually vertical, framed on the speaker (`../../footage/editing.md`), with captions
  burnt in (`../../captions/`), words on screen for the key line, and a short end card with the brand and the ask.
- **A title for each,** in the viewer's words, and post words for its platform (`../post-words.md`).

## The set

The shorts from one recording should differ: different ideas, different openings, a mix of lengths. Name them so
they sort in the order the user will post them.
