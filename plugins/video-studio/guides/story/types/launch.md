# Launches and promos

A film that markets, launches, reveals or promotes a product, a feature or a company: a promo, a feature reveal, a
demo, a site tour. A website is an information layout; a launch film is an emotional sequence.

## Find the product truth

- **The audience,** and what they want fixed or achieved.
- **The promise:** the one-line thesis of the film.
- **The product's role** in getting them there.
- **The proof:** features, interface moments, figures, customers, demos (real ones).
- **The ask.**

Build the order around the promise, not the website's page order or a feature list.

## Choose an arc

| Arc | Use when | Order |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem, agitate, solve** | the pain is known and urgent | hook, pain, the pain made sharper, a glimpse of the answer, the product, proof or demo, the ask |
| **The future** | it sells a new way, a category, a change | imagine it, name the product, the pain removed, how it works, the outcome, the ask |
| **The demo** | the interface explains itself | a question, the product, a demo, a second demo, trust, the ask |
| **Before, after, bridge** | it moves people from an old way to a better one | before, a glimpse of after, the product as the bridge, step one, step two, the wow, the ask |
| **Features into benefits** | it has many capabilities, or sells desire or status | a category hook, feature, benefit, feature, benefit, the climax, the ask |

Inside any arc, every feature is turned into what the viewer gets; raw features are never stacked. The brand's
register tunes the voice, not the arc: plain and low-key for a restrained business brand, short and punchy for a
bold launch, warm direct address for a friendly one, fewer and more aspirational words for a premium one.

## Demos are sequences

The value of an interface usually depends on a workflow, so a demo is three or more beats on the same surface (the
input, the response, the result, the benefit), never one isolated frame. Show the real product (`../../design/brand.md`,
"Real assets"); when a site tour or a "show it as it is" brief is the point, the real capture is the picture:
- Use the real screenshot as the base rather than rebuilding the site; overlay real parts at their measured places,
  or rebuild only the one component that has to move.
- A scroll travels over the full-page capture at its real size.
- If the capture is unusable (blocked, mostly empty), stop and ask for screenshots rather than inventing the page.

## Beats in their shape

Each scene pattern implies a way of saying its line. Write each beat's voiceover in the shape of the pattern it
leans towards (the patterns are in `../../motion/patterns/`), varying the patterns through the film:

| Beat | Pattern | The line's shape |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | `words-in-beats` | a punchy claim whose key word swaps or escalates |
| Hook | `typed-line` | an everyday thought, typed and corrected |
| Hook, problem | `pan-across-stations` | a march of milestones or pains, landing on "until now" or a knot |
| Hook, proof | `hub-and-satellites` | many tools or channels collapsing onto one centre: "in one place" |
| Hook | `takeover` | "Is it this? Or this?", then the answer crashes in |
| Hook, feature, ask | `prompt-and-answer` | the ask itself, typed; then one ask, one answer |
| Hook, benefit | `zoom-out-reveal` | a slow tease on the close-up, the line landing as the camera pulls back |
| Hook, benefit | `fixed-anchor` | one claim holding while who it's for, or what it works with, cycles |
| Hook, feature | `cursor-demo` | "everyone's already in here" over a live canvas; one workflow end to end |
| Hook, proof | `data-hero` | one figure counted as it's said; the numbers vouch, biggest last |
| Problem | `closing-in` | the tools pile up until they bury the viewer |
| Product | `logo-lockup` | the name alone, or nothing, as the mark assembles |
| Product, feature | `device-showcase` | the product doing its core loop: "…and that's it" |
| Product, benefit | `title-card` | a near-still card: the name, one line, then inside |
| Product, feature | `video-to-figure` | "watch it run, then look what it saved" |
| Feature | `assembling-grid` | "look how much it does": many capabilities at once |
| Feature | `side-by-side` | two capabilities in step: "X and Y, together" |
| Feature | `machine-at-work` | trigger, the work happening, the receipt, in the present tense |
| Feature | `live-edit` | short couplets: "do this, and that answers" |
| Feature | `scroll-to-deliverable` | "look how much happened", then the result |
| Feature, benefit | `camera-journey` | one calm, sweeping claim as the camera travels the result |
| Ask | `mark-to-button` | "This is [brand]. Start now." as the mark becomes the button |

If no pattern fits a beat, write the line plainly and compose the scene from techniques. Never bend the story to fit
a pattern.

## Music for a short launch

A track can match the mood and still open on a quiet build that drains the first seconds of a short film: compare its
opening with later sections and, when a later one starts stronger and cleanly, start there, with a short fade in and
a longer fade out. Check again whenever the cut's length changes, so the music never ends early or leaves silence at
the end (`../../audio/music.md`).
